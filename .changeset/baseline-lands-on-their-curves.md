Finishing a baseline FOR somebody lands on **their** curves.

A coach who completed a trainee's assessment was taken to their own Progress screen — empty
for most staff, and in any case not the page they had just asked for. The programme's
customer now rides along, so *Save it and see my numbers* opens the numbers of whoever the
baseline was for. No id still means mine, so a trainee keeps the bare `#/progress`.
