The person who wrote an exercise can now describe it.

`describe-exercise` refused every exercise that had an owner — even one you had just made
with `author-exercise` — and nothing else could write a private exercise's how-to. The
refusal said *only its owner can describe it* about a door that did not exist. Now an owned
exercise is described by its owner alone, read off the owner columns rather than the
`exercise → trainee` walk (performing an exercise makes that same edge, and doing a coach's
drill does not make its words yours). Library rows still need `library:publish`, an admin is
still refused on somebody else's, and the refusal arrives as a 403. No new keys or grants.
